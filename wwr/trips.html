🌿 Santa Lucía— Half-day A relaxing beginner trip on the Río Santa Lucía with gentle currents, birdlife, and scenic views. Perfect for families or first-time rafters who want a safe introduction to paddling.

🚣 Río Uruguay — Full day Experience moderate rapids and wide river landscapes on the Río Uruguay. A fun adventure for beginners with some confidence and anyone wanting excitement without extreme difficulty.
