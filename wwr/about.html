Whitewater Rafting Company

Whitewater Rafting Company is dedicated to creating safe, exciting river adventures across Uruguay for people of all experience levels. Our professional guides, quality equipment, and love for nature help every guest enjoy the river with confidence, teamwork, and unforgettable memories.

History

Our company began in 2010, with a small group of friends who wanted to share the beauty of outdoor adventure in Uruguay. What started as informal weekend trips with friends quickly grew into organized excursions as more people discovered the joy of rafting and exploring the country’s rivers and lagoons. With time, we invested in professional training, safety equipment, and guided programs to create experiences that were both exciting and secure.

As interest in eco-tourism and outdoor activities expanded, we continued developing new routes and welcoming visitors from across the region. Today, we remain committed to combining adventure with respect for nature, offering trips that celebrate the landscapes, teamwork, and relaxed spirit that make Uruguay unique.
